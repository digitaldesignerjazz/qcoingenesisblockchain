# QCoin Tokenomics

**Status:** Draft – 2026-08-23

## Purpose of QCoin

QCoin is the native economic unit of the Nexus / Avalon ecosystem.

Primary uses:
- Incentivize mesh participation (Yggdrasil / Avalon nodes)
- Reputation and staking for AI agents / peers
- Coordination rewards for Protocol Rounds
- Governance (future)
